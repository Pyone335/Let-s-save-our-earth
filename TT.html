🌍 Who Is Saving Our Earth?

Five students. Five places. Five small changes.
Tap a character to discover their eco-story!

🇮🇳 CALCUTTA, INDIA
🚲

Anika

TAP TO DISCOVER →
🚲 Anika — Calcutta, India

Anika used to go to school by car. Now, she bikes to school every day. She can save fuel, and the air will be cleaner if more people bike instead of driving.

🌱 Their change: Bike instead of drive.
🇬🇧 LONDON, UK
💡

Harry

TAP TO DISCOVER →
💡 Harry — London, UK

Harry used to use a lot of electricity. He now switches off the computer when he is not using it and switches off the lights too. He can save energy and protect the Earth.

🌱 Their change: Switch off unused lights and computers.
🇳🇿 WELLINGTON, NEW ZEALAND
💧

Emily

TAP TO DISCOVER →
💧 Emily — Wellington, New Zealand

Emily used to have long baths. Now, she takes a shower and turns off the tap while she is soaping herself. She wants to save water.

🌱 Their change: Save water while washing.
🇲🇲 YANGON, MYANMAR
♻️

May Mie

TAP TO DISCOVER →
♻️ May Mie — Yangon, Myanmar

May Mie used to throw away rubbish carelessly. Now, she sorts rubbish and puts different kinds in different dustbins. She also believes we should reduce, reuse or recycle waste.

🌱 Their change: Sort, reduce, reuse and recycle.
🇪🇹 ADDIS ABABA, ETHIOPIA
🌳

Abebe

TAP TO DISCOVER →
🌳 Abebe — Addis Ababa, Ethiopia

Abebe was not interested in growing trees in the past. Now, he grows plants and trees around his house and waters them every evening because trees produce oxygen.

🌱 Their change: Grow and care for trees.
💡 Think: Which student's change would you like to try?

💧 Problem: Wasting water.
♻️ Problem: Throwing rubbish away carelessly.
